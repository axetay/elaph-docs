# All Subcontractor Shipment Details

![All Subcontractor Shipment Details screenshot](../assets/screenshots/all-subcontractor-shipment-details.png)

This page lets you search, filter, and manage shipment records handled by subcontractors in the U-Go system. Operations staff use this to review subcontractor shipment details, verify assignments, and make updates before submitting requests. Access this page when you need to track or audit subcontractor work.

**URL:** `https://creatorapp.zoho.com/m.fahmy_ugologistics/u-go-trucking-management-system#Report:All_Subcontractor_Shipment_Details`

## How to Use

1. Select your preferred language from the language dropdown at the top if needed.
2. Use the checkboxes next to each shipment detail field to select which information you want to view or filter by (Shipment Type, Goods Description, Total Weight, Assigned Vehicle, Assigned Trailer, Assigned Driver, and Driver Cash Advance).
3. For each checked field, enter search terms in the text box that appears, or use the dropdown list to select from available options.
4. Adjust the volume range slider if you need to filter shipments by cargo volume.
5. Click 'Submit Request' to apply your filters and view matching subcontractor shipment records.
6. Review the results and make any necessary changes to shipment assignments or details.
7. Click 'Done' to save your work or use 'Add a Record' to enter a new subcontractor shipment.

## Tips

- You can check multiple filter fields at once to narrow down your search—for example, filter by both vehicle AND driver to find specific assignments.
- Use 'Import' to load subcontractor shipment data in bulk from an external file, and 'Remove Changes' to undo unsaved edits before you click 'Done'.
