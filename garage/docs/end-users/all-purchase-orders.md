# All Purchase Orders

![All Purchase Orders screenshot](../assets/screenshots/all-purchase-orders.png)

The All Purchase Orders page lets you search, filter, and manage all purchase orders in the U-Go Garage system. Operations staff use this page to find specific orders, check their status, and review costs and spare parts information. You'll visit this page regularly to track orders, verify deliveries, and manage procurement workflows.

**URL:** `https://creatorapp.zoho.com/m.fahmy_ugologistics/u-go-garage-maintenance#Report:All_Purchase_Orders`

## How to Use

1. Select your preferred language using the Language dropdown at the top if needed
2. Use the filter checkboxes on the left to select which columns you want to search by (Purchase Order ID, PO Date, Type, Status, Total Cost, or Spare Parts)
3. Enter your search criteria in the text fields that appear after checking each filter—for example, type an order number or select a status from the dropdown
4. For date ranges, enter the start date in the 'From' field and end date in the 'To' field
5. Click 'Done' to apply your filters and display matching purchase orders
6. Review the results and click 'Add a Record' if you need to create a new purchase order, or use other buttons to navigate to related forms or import/submit data

## Tips

- Check only the filter fields you actually need—fewer filters load results faster and reduce confusion
- Always verify the date range when searching by PO_Date to avoid missing recent or older orders
- Use the Main Forms, Master Data, and Support Forms buttons to navigate to related screens without losing your current search
