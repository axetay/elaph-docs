# All Auto PO Items

![All Auto PO Items screenshot](../assets/screenshots/all-auto-po-items.png)

The All Auto PO Items page displays all purchase order line items for vehicle maintenance parts across your garage. Procurement and maintenance staff use this page to track spare parts ordered, monitor quantities, view pricing, and manage the status of individual items within purchase orders.

**URL:** `https://creatorapp.zoho.com/m.fahmy_ugologistics/u-go-garage-maintenance#Report:All_Auto_PO_Items`

## How to Use

1. Open the All Auto PO Items page from the Procurement section to view the complete list of ordered parts.
2. Use the search fields (Auto PO Items ID, Spare Part, or Required Quantity) to find specific items or filter by supplier.
3. Select the checkboxes next to items you want to work with for bulk operations like editing or deletion.
4. Click Bulk Edit to update multiple items at once, or click on an individual row to view full details.
5. Use Print or Export to generate reports for inventory records or supplier communications.
6. Monitor the Status column to track whether items are pending, ordered, received, or completed.

## Tips

- Before bulk editing or deleting items, ensure you have the correct items selected—use filters to narrow the list and avoid accidental changes to unrelated purchase orders.
- Always verify quantities and pricing information with your supplier before finalizing orders; the Estimated Unit Price may change if supplier availability or market conditions shift.
